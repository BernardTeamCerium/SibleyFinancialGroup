Insurance & annuity products

Insurance and annuity products are offered through Sibley Financial Group, powered by Allied Elite Financial. Such products:

  • are not insured by the FDIC or any federal government agency;
  • are not deposits of, or obligations of, or guaranteed by, any bank or bank affiliate;
  • carry guarantees backed solely by the financial strength and claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company.

Annuities are long-term vehicles designed for retirement purposes. Withdrawals may be subject to surrender charges and, if taken prior to age 59½, may be subject to an additional 10% federal tax penalty. Optional riders may carry additional cost and may not be available in all states.

Fixed indexed annuities are insurance contracts. They are not investments in the market and do not directly participate in any stock, bond or equity investment. Index-linked crediting is subject to caps, spreads and participation rates that may change over the life of the contract. Please read any contract, illustration and accompanying disclosure carefully before purchase.

Privacy statement

What we collect. Through this website we collect only the information you choose to submit — typically your name, email address, telephone number and the details of your enquiry. Our hosting provider may also record standard technical data such as IP address and browser type.

How we use it. We use this information solely to respond to you and, if you become a client, to provide the services you have engaged us for. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Who we share it with. Information is shared only where it is necessary to serve you — for example with an insurance carrier when you apply for a product — or where we are required to do so by law.

Security. We maintain reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No method of internet transmission is entirely secure, however, and email in particular should not be treated as a confidential channel. Please do not send account numbers, Social Security numbers or other sensitive data through this website.

Your choices. You may ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to info@sibleyfinancialgroup.com.

Accessibility

Sibley Financial Group is committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technology. The site has been built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, with semantic structure, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus indicators, text alternatives for images and respect for reduced-motion preferences.

If you encounter a barrier on any page, please tell us at info@sibleyfinancialgroup.com or (985) 000-0000 and we will work to resolve it and to provide the information you need in another format.
