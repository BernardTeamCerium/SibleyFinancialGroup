Our process

Four steps, no surprises, and an exit at every stage

We have refined the same sequence for twenty-five years because it protects the client. You always know what happens next, what it costs, and what you are committing to — which, until the final step, is nothing.

Step 01

Introduction

About 45 minutes, in person or by phone

A relaxed conversation with no cost and no obligation. You describe what you have built, what concerns you, and what you want retirement to look like. We describe how we work and whether we are a fit.

  • Nothing is sold, quoted or recommended in this meeting.
  • You are not asked to bring statements or account numbers.
  • If we are not the right firm for you, we will say so and point you somewhere better.

Step 02

Discovery & Analysis

One to two weeks of work on our side

With your permission we gather the detail: retirement accounts, pensions, Social Security estimates, insurance policies, recent tax returns and your projected expenses. Then we stress-test it.

  • We model inflation, taxes, market losses and a longer-than-expected life.
  • We identify gaps between the income you will need and the income you have.
  • We look for risks you are carrying without being paid for them.

Step 03

Your Written Plan

Presented in a second meeting

You receive a written strategy in plain language: your income year by year, which portion is guaranteed, what remains exposed, the tax consequences, and exactly what we recommend changing.

  • Every recommendation states the reason behind it and the trade-off it carries.
  • The plan is yours to keep, question, and share with your CPA or attorney.
  • You are free to take it and implement none of it.

Step 04

Implementation & Review

Ongoing, for as long as you want us

If you decide to proceed, we handle the applications, transfers and paperwork, and confirm everything landed where it should. Then we keep the plan current.

  • Scheduled reviews to check the plan against real results, not assumptions.
  • Updates when tax law, your health, or your circumstances change.
  • A direct line to your advisor — not a call centre.

Our commitment

You can stop at any point

There is no stage of this process where you owe us anything or are obliged to continue. That is deliberate. Advice given under pressure is worth exactly what it costs you, and we would rather earn a client for twenty years than close one this quarter.
