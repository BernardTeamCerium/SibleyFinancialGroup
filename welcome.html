What happens next

Your first ninety days

No surprises, and nothing you need to chase.

  • Within a few days

    Your welcome box arrives

    Keep the portfolio somewhere your family can find it. If anything is missing or damaged, tell us and we will replace it.

  • Week one

    Your plan documents land

    Confirmations from any carrier arrive directly from them. We check every one against what we recommended, so you do not have to.

  • Around day thirty

    A short check-in call

    Ten minutes. We confirm everything is in force, answer whatever has occurred to you since, and make sure your beneficiaries are recorded correctly.

    We meet on a set schedule to test the plan against real results and update it as tax law, markets and your life change. You will never have to chase us for it.
