Esse Quam Videri

To be, rather than to seem.

The motto on our crest is not decoration. It is the test we apply to every recommendation we make.

A retirement plan can look impressive and still fail the person who owns it. Ours are built the other way around: plain language, honest arithmetic, and strategies that hold up in the years that do not go according to plan — the market correction, the health event, the tax bill nobody warned you about.

We are an independent firm. We are not selling you a house product because it is the one on the shelf. We start with what you need retirement income to do, and work backward to the tools that do it.

How it works

A clear path from uncertainty to a written plan

No jargon, no surprises, and no obligation at any point. Most clients know after the first conversation whether we are the right fit.

Step 01

Introduction

A relaxed, no-obligation conversation. You tell us what you have, what worries you, and what you want retirement to look like. We tell you plainly whether we can help.

Step 02

Discovery & Analysis

We gather the details — statements, pensions, Social Security estimates, tax returns, policies — and stress-test them against inflation, taxes, market losses and longevity.

Step 03

Your Written Plan

You receive a clear, written strategy: where your income comes from each year, what is protected, what is exposed, and exactly what we recommend changing and why.

Step 04

Implementation & Review

We put the plan in place, handle the paperwork, and meet with you on a set schedule to keep it current as tax law, markets and your life change.
