A note from Troy

Thank you. Genuinely.

Twenty-five years in, the part of this work I did not expect is how much of it stops feeling like work. You call about a grandchild’s college fund and we end up talking about the grandchild. That is not a client list. That is a group of people I am glad to know.

So once a year we stop talking about markets entirely, get everyone together, and watch some football. No presentation. No pitch. No one is going to corner you by the concession stand about an annuity. Just a thank-you for trusting us with something that matters.

Come hungry, and come loud.

Bring a friend

Know someone with a retirement question they keep putting off?

Bring them with you. There is an extra seat in our section for a guest of yours, and no agenda attached to it.

Most people we meet were introduced by someone already sitting in our office. Not because they were sold on us — because a friend said you should talk to Troy. If someone in your life is five years out and quietly worried, a football game is a far easier place to start that conversation than a conference room.

They are your guest for the day, not a prospect. If they want to talk, we will talk. If they just want to watch the game, that is a good day too.

  • They are retiring in the next five to ten years and are not certain what their income will actually be.
  • They just changed jobs or retired and have a 401(k) sitting where they left it.
  • They recently lost a spouse and are facing decisions they never expected to make alone.
  • They are selling a business and turning it into personal retirement security.
  • They are carrying more market risk than they can sleep on but do not know what else to do.

Just add their name when you register so we can print a badge and hold a seat.
